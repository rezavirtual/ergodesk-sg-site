How to Mount Dual Monitors on Small Study Desks: Singapore Ergonomics Guide

When working on a compact 100cm or 120cm study table, monitor stock stands occupy nearly a third of your usable surface area, creating clutter and forcing awkward keyboard angles.

By attaching a heavy-duty gas-spring monitor arm, you elevate your displays to natural eye level—eliminating forward neck strain while reclaiming up to 85% of your desk space.

Mounting Tips for Singapore Desks

  • Check Tabletop Clearance: Ensure your desk has at least 5cm of rear lip clearance for the C-clamp base.
  • VESA Compatibility: Check whether your monitors use 75x75mm or 100x100mm mounting patterns.
  • Cable Routing: Use the arm's internal channels to route HDMI and power cables cleanly.
